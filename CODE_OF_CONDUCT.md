# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Prismatic a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing
viewpoints, and accepting responsibility. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment,
insults, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, and other conduct inappropriate in
a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile.
Maintainers will investigate promptly, protect reporter privacy, and may correct,
warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban participants according to impact.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
